to a network.

Three specialist agents can produce three correct reports and still fail to recognise the key trade-off. The next step is bulding a system that works agentically, not programmatically.

A short framing before the working prototype
How it works today

A relay race

Each agent completes a chapter and hands over. Findings move one way, leading to confusion and .

A late finding can be correct and still arrive too late to reshape the recommendation.
The cost

Good at coverage.
Poor at judgement.

A pipeline covers every topic. It does not own the trade-off between them.

No one owns the trade-off

Market finds demand. Competitive finds crowding. Product finds feasibility. The call that matters sits between those findings, not inside any one report.

Late findings land too late

A constraint found at the end cannot redirect work that has already been completed.

Contradictions become homework

The pipeline prints both sides and leaves leadership to reconcile them.

Every chapter can be individually correct and the recommendation still be wrong.
A concrete example

One coffee decision.
Three valid answers.

Italy is locked. One premium SKU. Existing equipment wherever possible.

Market Familiar espresso has the strongest demand.
Product The same option is easiest to manufacture.
Competitive It also enters the most saturated territory.
→
Pipeline outcome Three true statements.

One unresolved choice is handed to the reader.

Orchestrator response Name the tension. Ask the priority. Re-plan the work.

The second round exists because of what the first round found.

The demo will resolve this decision. The point is not the coffee — it is whether the system can turn conflict into the next useful question.
The shift

One owner for the decision

Specialists keep domain authority. The Orchestrator keeps canonical state and owns reconciliation.

Evidence meets in one governed state. Contradictions cannot hide between separate chapters.
The loop

The loop has a human boundary

Round two changes because of round one. A human steps in only when evidence cannot settle a material preference.

System may

Reconcile evidence and issue targeted follow-up work.

Human decides

Preference-sensitive choices that change the strategy.

System never

Silently changes a locked constraint.

Every revised brief names its trigger. Every consequential choice has an owner.
The product

A governed decision loop.
Not more reports.

The product is the controlled movement from specialist evidence to a recommendation leadership can inspect.

DECISION QUALITY

Conflict changes the work

The system compares specialist evidence, names the tension, and targets the next round at what remains unresolved.

DECISION TRACE

Every change has a cause

Canonical state, causal triggers, human escalation, and residual risks make the recommendation auditable from brief to outcome.

The value is not agent count. It is a decision process that can explain what changed, why, and who decided.
The path

Proof now. Pilot next.

Keep the interaction model stable. Change the evidence source only after the loop earns the right to go live.

V1 · THIS PROTOTYPE

Prove the loop

Three specialists, deterministic findings, and a real plan → report → reconcile → re-plan interaction.

V2 · BOUNDED PILOT

Run one decision live

Use live specialist research on one real opportunity. Measure decision quality, traceability, and time against the current linear process.

LATER · EXPAND

Add specialists carefully

Bring in Revenue, Go-to-Market, Unit Economics, and others only after the pilot proves the decision loop adds value.

V2 is a controlled test of one claim: linked specialists produce a better, more inspectable decision.
